Goal

Control 5 connected provinces first (8 on the big board).

Rivers split the board into provinces of 2×2 squares. A province is yours when only your soldiers are in it. Connected means joined by your bridges.

You have 36 soldiers and 8 bridges for the whole game (big board: 54 and 14).

↑ Top

Your turn

1. Roll the dice: two squares light up. Pick one.

2. Not happy? Re-roll once. The new roll replaces the old one.

3. Make one move on the lit square.

No move possible? Re-roll. Still nothing? Tap Pass.

↑ Top

Land

New soldiers from your camp land only on the lit square: empty, or your own base.

↑ Top

Move

Move any number of soldiers to a neighbouring square (not diagonal).

Lit square in your territory: use any of your bases. Otherwise: only a base touching the lit square.

↑ Top

Attack

A move onto enemy soldiers. Each attacker kills one defender.

↑ Top

Withdraw

Take soldiers back to camp, from any base of your territory.

↑ Top

Transport

Send soldiers along a straight line through your territory, in one move.

↑ Top

Bridges

A river can only be crossed on a bridge. Only its owner can cross it.

Crossing with no bridge builds yours. Your supply is limited.

Building a bridge and moving over it is one move.
↑ Top

Territory

Your bases + the squares of provinces you control, joined by your bridges.

A lit square in your territory lets you play from any base in it. You can never land in enemy territory. A province with both colours is nobody's.
